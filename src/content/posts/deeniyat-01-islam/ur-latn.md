## Khulaasa

Maulana Maududi kitaab ki shuruaat ek saada baat se karte hain: *Islam* ka matlab Khuda ki farmaan-bardaari hai, aur kaayenaat ki har cheez pehle hi isi tarah jee rahi hai. Is maamle mein asli ikhtiyaar sirf insaan ko mila hai. Yeh baab isi ikhtiyaar ko samjhaata hai: inkaar (kufr) se kya khoya jaata hai aur maanne (Islam) se kya milta hai.

## 1. Is ka naam "Islam" kyun hai?

Zyaadatar mazhabon ka naam kisi shakhs ya qaum par rakha gaya hai: Isaaiyat Hazrat Isa ke naam par, Budh mat Mahaatma Budh ke naam par, Zartushti mazhab Zartusht ke naam par, aur Yahoodiyat Yahooda ke qabeele ke naam par. Islam in mein se kisi par nahin. Is ka naam ek **khoobi** batata hai: Khuda ke aage jhukna aur us ka hukm maanna.

Is se do baatein maaloom hoti hain:

- Islam kisi ek aadmi ka banaaya hua nahin, aur na kisi ek qaum ki milkiyat hai.
- Har zamaane aur har mulk mein jis kisi mein yeh khoobi thi, woh *Muslim* tha.

## 2. Har cheez pehle hi farmaan-bardaar hai

Sooraj, chaand aur sitaare apne raaste se baal baraabar nahin hatte. Paani, hawa, roshni aur garmi apne apne qaaide par chalte hain. Paude aur jaanwar apne tay qaanoon ke mutaabiq paida hote, badhte aur marte hain. Khud insaan ka jism bhi saans lene, khaana hazm karne aur khoon ke daudne mein isi atal nizaam ka paaband hai.

Yeh sab ek hi qaanoon ke tahat hai jo ek hi Haakim ne banaaya hai. Aur chunke Islam ka matlab Khuda ka hukm maanna hai, is liye saari kaayenaat ka "mazhab" Islam hai. Jo shakhs Khuda ka inkaar karta hai woh bhi apne jism ke lihaaz se muslim hai: us ka dil dhadakta hai aur phephde saans lete hain to Khuda hi ke qaanoon ke mutaabiq.

## 3. Insaan ke do pehloo

1. **Majboori waala pehloo.** Baaqi sab cheezon ki tarah hum bhi qudrat ke qaanoon ke paaband hain aur is mein hamaara koi dakhal nahin.
2. **Ikhtiyaar waala pehloo.** Baaqi sab cheezon ke ulat hum soch sakte hain, raaye bana sakte hain aur chun sakte hain. Hum apni marzi ke kaamon mein Khuda ki hidaayat par chal sakte hain, ya apne banaaye hue qaaidon par.

Ikhtiyaar ke is daayre mein insaan do tarah ke ho jaate hain:

- Jo apne paida karne waale ko pehchaan kar apni marzi se us ka hukm maane, woh **poora Muslim** hai. Ab woh usi Khuda ka hukm jaan boojh kar maanta hai jis ka anjaane mein pehle se maan raha tha. Woh kaayenaat ke saath mel mein hai aur zameen par Khuda ka *Khaleefa* (naaib) hai.
- Jo apni aql ko Khuda ke inkaar mein lagaaye aur apni marzi ke kaamon mein us ka hukm maanne se inkaar kare, woh **kaafir** hai.

## 4. Kufr: haqeeqat par parda daalna

*Kufr* ke lafzi maani hain "chhupaana". Kaafir ne apni fitrat par jahaalat ka parda daal diya hai. Woh Islam ki fitrat par paida hua, Islam par chalti duniya mein rehta hai, phir bhi us ke khilaaf sochta aur jeeta hai. Maulana is ke nuqsaan yun bataate hain:

| Kufr… | Is liye ke… |
|---|---|
| **Jahaalat hai** | Woh kaayenaat ka itna bada aur samajhdaari se bana nizaam dekhta hai magar us ke banaane waale ko nahin pehchaanta. Jo ilm apni pehli buniyaad hi se khaali ho, woh kabhi seedha raasta nahin paata. |
| **Zulm hai** | Zulm ka matlab hai kisi cheez se us ki fitrat ke khilaaf kaam lena. Kaafir apne dil, apne jism ke hisson aur duniya ki cheezon ko usi marzi ke khilaaf istemaal karta hai jis ki khidmat ke liye woh bani hain. |
| **Naashukri aur baghaawat hai** | Insaan ke paas jo kuch hai, yahan tak ke aulaad ke liye maa baap ki mohabbat bhi, Khuda hi ki di hui hai. Us ki ne'maton ko usi ke khilaaf istemaal karna us naukar ke dhoke se bhi bura hai jo apne khilaane waale maalik ko dhoka de. |
| **Apna nuqsaan hai, Khuda ka nahin** | Khuda ki saltanat ki koi hadd nahin aur woh kisi ka mohtaaj nahin. Inkaar karne waala sirf apni tabaahi ka saamaan karta hai. |
| **Dono jahaanon mein naakaami hai** | Bigda hua ilm akhlaaq, samaaj, kamaai ke tareeqe aur siyaasat sab ko bigaad deta hai. Aur aakhirat mein har woh cheez jis ka us ne ghalat istemaal kiya, us ke khilaaf gawaahi degi. |

## 5. Islam kya deta hai

**Aazaadi ek imtihaan hai.** Khuda ne hamein aql, achchhe aur bure ki pehchaan aur chunne ki aazaadi di hai. Isi se yeh imtihaan asli banta hai: jis imtihaan mein jawaab zabardasti likhwaaye jaayein woh kuch saabit nahin karta. Jo inkaar ki taaqat rakhte hue bhi apni marzi se hukm maanna chune, woh kaamyaab hai.

**Sahi raah dikhaane waala ilm.** Jo Khuda ko jaanta hai woh jaanta hai ke ilm kahan se shuru hota hai aur kahan le jaata hai:

- **falsafe** mein woh khoj karta hai magar shak mein doobta nahin;
- **science** mein woh qudrat ke qaanoon dhoondhta hai magar unhein loot maar aur qabze ke liye istemaal nahin karta, aur har nayi khoj us ka shukr badhaati hai;
- **tareekh, economics, siyaasat aur qaanoon** mein woh kisi se kam mehnat nahin karta, magar us ka maqsad insaaf, sab ki bhalaai, aur hukoomat ko Khuda ki amaanat samajhna hota hai.

**Kirdaar.** Woh har cheez ko, yahan tak ke apne jism ko bhi, Khuda ki amaanat samajhta hai. Is liye apne dil, aankh, kaan, zabaan aur haath ki hifaazat karta hai; haraam khaane se bhooka rehna behtar samajhta hai; jhooth aur ghalat ke aage kabhi nahin jhukta; aur sachchaai ko har faayde se zyada azeez rakhta hai.

Maulana ke nazdeek aisa shakhs sab se zyada **izzat waala** hai (Khuda ke siwa kisi ke aage nahin jhukta), sab se zyada **taaqatwar** hai (Khuda ke siwa kisi se nahin darta), sab se zyada **ameer** hai (thode par khush rehna sab se badi daulat hai), sab se zyada **pyaara** hai (har ek ka haq ada karta hai) aur sab se zyada **bharose ke laayak** hai (imaandaar hai kyunke Khuda dekh raha hai). Woh is duniya mein bhi kaamyaab hai, aur aakhirat mein Khuda ki amaanat poori karne ka in'aam paayega.

**Islam insaan ka qudrati deen hai.** Yeh kisi ek qaum ka nahin. Tareekh mein har sachcha aur Khuda ko pehchaanne waala insaan isi par chala, chaahe us ki zabaan mein is ka naam kuch bhi raha ho.

## Aham alfaaz

- **Islam:** Khuda ke aage jhukna aur us ka hukm maanna.
- **Muslim:** Khuda ka farmaan-bardaar.
- **Kufr / Kaafir:** haqeeqat ko chhupaana / chhupaane waala.
- **Fitrat:** woh asli mizaaj jo Khuda ne har cheez mein rakha hai, aur woh Islam hai.
- **Zulm:** kisi cheez se us ki fitrat ke khilaaf kaam lena.
- **Khaleefa:** zameen par Khuda ka naaib.

## Ghaur karne ki baatein

- Agar hamaara jism hamaari marzi ke baghair hi Khuda ka hukm maan raha hai, to zindagi ke us hisse mein hukm maanne ka kya matlab hai jahan hum *khud* chunte hain?
- Zindagi ke kin maamlon mein hum aise rehte hain jaise jo kuch hamaare paas hai us ke maalik hum hain, amaanatdaar nahin?
- Maulana kehte hain ke thode par khush rehna sab se badi daulat hai. Hamaari zindagi mein is ki sab se zyada zaroorat kahan hai?
