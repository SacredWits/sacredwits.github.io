## Khulaasa

Maulana Maududi kitaab ka aaghaaz ek saada baat se karte hain: *Islam* ka matlab Khuda ki farmaan-bardaari hai, aur kaayenaat ki har cheez pehle hi isi tarah jee rahi hai. Is maamle mein haqeeqi ikhtiyaar sirf insaan ko mila hai. Yeh baab isi ikhtiyaar ko samjhaata hai: inkaar (kufr) se kya khoya jaata hai aur maanne (Islam) se kya milta hai.

## 1. Is ka naam "Islam" kyun hai?

Aksar mazaahib ka naam kisi shakhs ya qaum par rakha gaya hai: Isaaiyat Hazrat Isa ke naam par, Budh mat Mahaatma Budh ke naam par, Zartushti mazhab Zartusht ke naam par, aur Yahoodiyat Yahooda ke qabeele ke naam par. Islam in mein se kisi par nahin. Is ka naam ek **sifat** batata hai: Khuda ke aage jhukna aur us ki ita'at karna.

Is se do baatein maaloom hoti hain:

- Islam kisi ek aadmi ka banaaya hua nahin, aur na kisi ek qaum ki milkiyat hai.
- Har zamaane aur har mulk mein jis kisi mein yeh sifat thi, woh *Muslim* tha.

## 2. Har cheez pehle hi farmaan-bardaar hai

Sooraj, chaand aur sitaare apne raaste se baal baraabar nahin hatte. Paani, hawa, roshni aur haraarat apne apne qaaide par chalte hain. Paude aur jaanwar apne muqarrar qaanoon ke mutaabiq paida hote, badhte aur marte hain. Khud insaan ka jism bhi saans lene, hazm karne aur khoon ki gardish mein isi atal nizaam ka paaband hai.

Yeh sab ek hi qaanoon ke tahat hai jo ek hi haakim ne banaaya hai. Aur chunke Islam ka matlab Khuda ki ita'at hai, is liye saari kaayenaat ka "mazhab" Islam hai. Jo shakhs Khuda ka inkaar karta hai woh bhi apne jism ke lihaaz se muslim hai: us ka dil dhadakta hai aur phephde saans lete hain to Khuda hi ke qaanoon ki pairavi mein.

## 3. Insaan ki do haisiyatein

1. **Majboori ki haisiyat.** Baaqi sab cheezon ki tarah hum bhi qaanoon-e-qudrat ke paaband hain aur is mein hamaara koi dakhal nahin.
2. **Ikhtiyaar ki haisiyat.** Baaqi sab cheezon ke barkhilaaf hum soch sakte hain, raaye qaayam kar sakte hain aur chun sakte hain. Hum apne ikhtiyaari kaamon mein Khuda ki hidaayat par chal sakte hain, ya apne banaaye hue qaaidon par.

Ikhtiyaar ke is daayre mein insaan do girohon mein bat jaate hain:

- Jo apne Khaaliq ko pehchaan kar apni marzi se us ki ita'at kare, woh **poora Muslim** hai. Ab woh usi Khuda ki shu'oori ita'at karta hai jis ki be-shu'oori mein pehle se kar raha tha. Woh kaayenaat se ham-aahang hai aur zameen par Khuda ka *Khaleefa* (naaib) hai.
- Jo apni aql ko Khuda ke inkaar mein lagaaye aur apne ikhtiyaari kaamon mein us ki ita'at se inkaar kare, woh **kaafir** hai.

## 4. Kufr: haqeeqat par parda daalna

*Kufr* ke lafzi maani hain "chhupaana". Kaafir ne apni fitrat par jahaalat ka parda daal diya hai. Woh Islam ki fitrat par paida hua, Islam par chalti duniya mein rehta hai, phir bhi us ke khilaaf sochta aur jeeta hai. Musannif is ke nuqsaanaat yun bayaan karte hain:

| Kufr… | Is liye ke… |
|---|---|
| **Jahaalat hai** | Woh kaayenaat ka azeem aur ba-hikmat nizaam dekhta hai magar us ke banaane waale ko nahin pehchaanta. Jo ilm apni pehli buniyaad hi se khaali ho, woh kabhi seedha raasta nahin paata. |
| **Zulm hai** | Zulm ka matlab hai kisi cheez se us ki fitrat ke khilaaf kaam lena. Kaafir apne dil, apne aaza aur duniya ki cheezon ko usi marzi ke khilaaf istemaal karta hai jis ki khidmat ke liye woh bani hain. |
| **Naashukri aur baghaawat hai** | Insaan ke paas jo kuch hai, yahan tak ke aulaad ke liye maa baap ki mohabbat bhi, Khuda hi ki di hui hai. Us ki ne'maton ko usi ke khilaaf istemaal karna us naukar ki be-wafaai se bhi badtar hai jo apne khilaane waale aaqa se dagha kare. |
| **Apna nuqsaan hai, Khuda ka nahin** | Khuda ki saltanat ki koi hadd nahin aur woh kisi ka mohtaaj nahin. Inkaar karne waala sirf apni tabaahi ka saamaan karta hai. |
| **Dono jahaanon mein naakaami hai** | Bigda hua ilm akhlaaq, mu'aasharat, ma'eeshat aur siyaasat sab ko bigaad deta hai. Aur aakhirat mein har woh cheez jis ka us ne ghalat istemaal kiya, us ke khilaaf gawaahi degi. |

## 5. Islam kya deta hai

**Aazaadi ek imtihaan hai.** Khuda ne hamein aql, neki aur badi ki pehchaan aur chunne ki aazaadi di hai. Isi se yeh imtihaan haqeeqi banta hai: jis imtihaan mein jawaab zabardasti likhwaaye jaayein woh kuch saabit nahin karta. Jo inkaar ki taaqat rakhte hue bhi apni marzi se ita'at chune, woh kaamyaab hai.

**Simt rakhne waala ilm.** Jo Khuda ko jaanta hai woh jaanta hai ke ilm kahan se shuru hota hai aur kahan le jaata hai:

- **falsafe** mein woh tahqeeq karta hai magar shukook mein doobta nahin;
- **science** mein woh qudrat ke qawaaneen daryaaft karta hai magar unhein loot maar aur tasallut ke liye istemaal nahin karta, aur har daryaaft us ka shukr badhaati hai;
- **tareekh, ma'aashiyaat, siyaasat aur qaanoon** mein woh kisi se kam mehnat nahin karta, magar us ka maqsad adl, sab ki bhalaai, aur hukoomat ko Khuda ki amaanat samajhna hota hai.

**Kirdaar.** Woh har cheez ko, yahan tak ke apne jism ko bhi, Khuda ki amaanat samajhta hai. Is liye apne dil, aankh, kaan, zabaan aur haath ki hifaazat karta hai; haraam khaane par bhook ko tarjeeh deta hai; baatil ke aage kabhi nahin jhukta; aur sachchaai ko har faayde se zyada azeez rakhta hai.

Musannif ke nazdeek aisa shakhs sab se zyada **ba-izzat** hai (Khuda ke siwa kisi ke aage nahin jhukta), sab se zyada **taaqatwar** hai (Khuda ke siwa kisi se nahin darta), sab se zyada **ghani** hai (qanaa'at sab se badi daulat hai), sab se zyada **mehboob** hai (har ek ka haq ada karta hai) aur sab se zyada **qaabil-e-e'tibaar** hai (imaandaar hai kyunke Khuda dekh raha hai). Woh is duniya mein bhi kaamyaab hai, aur aakhirat mein Khuda ki amaanat poori karne ka in'aam paayega.

**Islam insaan ka fitri deen hai.** Yeh kisi ek qaum ka nahin. Tareekh mein har sachcha aur Khuda-shanaas insaan isi par chala, chaahe us ki zabaan mein is ka naam kuch bhi raha ho.

## Aham istilaahaat

- **Islam:** Khuda ke aage jhukna aur us ki ita'at karna.
- **Muslim:** Khuda ka farmaan-bardaar.
- **Kufr / Kaafir:** haqeeqat ko chhupaana / chhupaane waala.
- **Fitrat:** woh mizaaj jo Khuda ne har cheez mein rakha hai, aur woh Islam hai.
- **Zulm:** kisi cheez se us ki fitrat ke khilaaf kaam lena.
- **Khaleefa:** zameen par Khuda ka naaib.

## Ghaur karne ki baatein

- Agar hamaara jism hamaari marzi ke baghair hi Khuda ki ita'at kar raha hai, to zindagi ke us hisse mein ita'at ka kya matlab hai jahan hum *khud* chunte hain?
- Zindagi ke kin maamlaat mein hum aise baratte hain jaise jo kuch hamaare paas hai us ke maalik hum hain, amaanatdaar nahin?
- Musannif kehte hain ke qanaa'at sab se badi daulat hai. Hamaari zindagi mein is ki sab se zyada zaroorat kahan hai?
