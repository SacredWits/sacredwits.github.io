## Khulaasa

Pehle baab mein yeh saaf hua ke Islam Khuda ka hukm maanne ka naam hai. Doosra baab yeh poochhta hai ke yeh hukm maanna mumkin kis cheez se hota hai. Maulana Maududi ka jawaab hai: aisa ilm jis par yaqeen ho, aur yahi *imaan* hai. Imaan beej hai aur ita'at darakht. Chunke hamaari apni aql is ilm tak yaqeen ke saath nahin pahunch sakti, is liye Khuda ne yeh ilm apne Paighambaron ke zariye bheja, aur un par bharosa karna waisa hi hai jaisa hum roz kisi achchhe doctor par karte hain.

## 1. Ita'at kis cheez par tiki hai

Jis par yaqeen na ho us ka hukm nahin maana ja sakta. Maulana chaar baatein bataate hain jin ka ilm aur yaqeen hue baghair Khuda ki ita'at jad nahin pakadti:

| Yaqeen hona chaahiye… | Is liye ke… |
|---|---|
| **Ke Khuda maujood hai** | Jis ke hone hi mein shak ho us ka hukm koi nahin maanta. |
| **Us ki sifaat ka** | Jo jaanta hai ke woh ek hai, woh kisi aur ke aage nahin jhukega; jo jaanta hai ke woh sab kuch dekhta aur sunta hai, woh us ki naafarmaani se rukega. |
| **Us ke qaanoon ka** | Yeh maaloom hona chaahiye ke use kya pasand hai aur kya naapasand, aur yeh yaqeen ke yahi us ka qaanoon hai. Ilm na ho to pairavi kis ki? Shak ho to pairavi jam kar nahin hoti. |
| **Aakhirat ka** | Agar maut hi sab kuch khatm kar deti ho to hukm maanna aur na maanna dono be-nateeja lagte hain. Sirf hisaab, inaam aur saza ka yaqeen hi insaan ko ita'at ki mehnat uthaane aur lubhaane waali buraaiyan chhodne par taiyaar karta hai. |

Yahan asli dushman shak hai. Insaan poori tarah usi kaam mein lagta hai jis ke faayde ka use *yaqeen* ho, aur usi cheez se bachta hai jis ke nuqsaan ka use *yaqeen* ho.

## 2. Imaan kya hai

Yahi ilm jab yaqeen ke saath ho to **imaan** hai: jaanna aur maanna. Jis ke paas yeh ho woh **momin** hai, aur us ka qudrati nateeja yeh hai ke woh **Muslim** yaani farmaan-bardaar ban jaata hai.

Maulana ki misaal **beej aur darakht** ki hai. Beej ke baghair darakht paida hi nahin hota. Magar beej boya jaaye tab bhi zameen kharaab ho ya mausam theek na ho to darakht kamzor nikal sakta hai. Isi tarah kisi ke dil mein sachcha imaan ho aur phir bhi iraade ki kamzori, kami waali tarbiyat ya buri sohbat ki wajah se woh amal mein kamzor reh jaaye.

## 3. Insaanon ki chaar qismein

| | Imaan | Amal | Un ka darja |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hai | Poori ita'at | **Sachche Musalmaan.** Khuda ki naapasand baat se aise bachte hain jaise koi aag se haath bachaata hai, aur us ki pasand ki baat us shauq se karte hain jaise koi rozi kamaane ke liye mehnat karta hai. |
| 2 | Hai | Kami hai | **Phir bhi Musalmaan, magar neeche darje ke.** Naafarmaani karein to mujrim hain aur saza ke haqdaar, magar baaghi nahin, kyunke woh baadshaah ko baadshaah aur us ke qaanoon ko qaanoon maante hain. |
| 3 | Nahin | Dikhne mein achchha | **Asal mein baaghi.** Imaan ke baghair nek amal Khuda ki ita'at nahin, jaise baadshaah ko na maanne waala sirf is liye wafaadaar nahin ho jaata ke us ne abhi koi qaanoon nahin toda. |
| 4 | Nahin | Bura | **Sab se bure:** baaghi bhi aur fasaad phailaane waale bhi. |

Sabaq yeh hai ke sab kuch imaan par tika hai. Islam, poora ho ya adhoora, sirf imaan ke beej se ugta hai. Jahan imaan nahin wahan kufr hai, yaani Khuda se baghaawat, chhoti ho ya badi.

## 4. Yeh ilm kahan se milta hai

Kaayenaat ek samajhdaar banaane waale ki nishaaniyon se bhari hai, magar akeli insaani aql ne baar baar unhein ghalat padha:

- kisi ne do khuda maane, kisi ne teen, kisi ne anginat;
- kisi ne khudaai ko baant diya: baarish ka ek khuda, hawa ka ek, aag ka ek;
- marne ke baad ke baare mein kisi ne kaha maut hi anjaam hai, kisi ne kaha insaan baar baar isi duniya mein janam leta hai;
- aur apni aql se zindagi ka poora qaanoon banaana is se bhi mushkil hai.

Behtareen dimaagh bhi barson ki mehnat ke baad sirf ek raaye tak pahunch sakta tha, yaqeen tak nahin. Agar Khuda insaan ko sirf us ki aql ke hawaale kar deta to bahut thode log sach tak pahunchte.

Is ke bajaaye Khuda ne **apni meharbaani se** insaanon hi mein se kuch log chune, unhein apni zaat, apne qaanoon aur aakhirat ka sahi ilm diya aur hukm diya ke yeh ilm doosron tak pahunchaayein. Yahi **Paighambar** hain. Jis zariye se Khuda ne unhein yeh ilm diya woh **wahi** hai, aur jis kitaab mein yeh ilm hai woh **Allah ki Kitaab** hai.

Ab imtihaan yeh hai ke Paighambar ki paak zindagi aur oonchi taleem dekh kar hum sach ko pehchaante aur maante hain ya nahin. Jis ne maana woh kaamyaab hua. Jis ne inkaar kiya us ne saabit kar diya ke woh sach ko pehchaanne ki salaahiyat kho chuka hai, aur woh Khuda, us ke qaanoon aur aakhirat ka sahi ilm kabhi nahin pa sakega.

## 5. Ghaib par imaan (Imaan bil-Ghaib)

Is usool par hum har roz chalte hain:

- Beemaar hote hain to khud ilaaj nahin karte, doctor ke paas jaate hain; us ki degree, tajurbe aur theek hue mareezon par bharosa kar ke us ki dawa us ke bataaye tareeqe se lete hain.
- Qaanooni maamle mein vakeel ki, padhaai mein ustaad ki, aur anjaan raaste par raasta jaanne waale ki baat maante hain.

Har maamle mein hum majboor hain ke jo baat khud nahin jaante us mein kisi jaanne waale par bharosa karein. Yahi **ghaib par imaan** hai: jo hum khud maaloom nahin kar sakte, woh kisi jaanne waale aur bharose ke laayak shakhs se le lena.

Khuda ki zaat aur sifaat, us ke hukm par kaam karne waale farishte, us ki marzi ke mutaabiq jeene ka tareeqa, aur aakhirat ki haqeeqat, yeh sab hum khud nahin dekh sakte. Yeh sab hum Paighambar se seekhte hain, jin ki sachchaai, imaandaari, Khuda ka dar, paak zindagi aur samajhdaari hamein yaqeen dilaati hai ke woh jo kehte hain sach hai. Isi liye Khuda ki ita'at ke liye ghaib par imaan zaroori hai: Paighambar ke baghair sahi ilm nahin milta, aur sahi ilm ke baghair koi Islam ke raaste par theek nahin chal sakta.

## Aham alfaaz

- **Imaan:** yaqeen ke saath jaanna aur maanna.
- **Momin:** imaan rakhne waala.
- **Muslim:** Khuda ka farmaan-bardaar; ita'at imaan ka phal hai.
- **Wahi:** woh zariya jis se Khuda apne Paighambaron ko ilm deta hai.
- **Kitaab-Allah:** Allah ki Kitaab, jis mein yeh wahi mehfooz hai.
- **Imaan bil-Ghaib:** an-dekhi haqeeqaton par imaan; jo hum khud nahin jaan sakte use kisi bharose ke laayak zariye se maanna.

## Ghaur karne ki baatein

- Chaar yaqeenon (Khuda, us ki sifaat, us ka qaanoon, aakhirat) mein se kaun sa hum mein sab se kamzor hai, aur use kaise mazboot kiya jaaye?
- Beej aur darakht ki misaal mein hamaari zindagi ki woh "zameen" kaun si hai jo imaan ko badhne mein madad deti ya rokti hai?
- Hum doctor aur ustaad par roz bina jhijhak bharosa karte hain. Paighambar ﷺ par kam se kam utna hi bharosa karne ka kya matlab hoga?
