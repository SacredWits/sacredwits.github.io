## Khulaasa

Deebaacha mukhtasar hai, magar is mein poori kitaab ka falsafa simat aaya hai. Musannif ki baat yeh hai ke deeni taleem mein yeh batane se pehle ke Islam *kya* hai aur use *kyun* maana jaaye, yeh sikhaya ja raha tha ke us par amal *kaise* kiya jaaye. Deeniyat isi tarteeb ko badalne ki koshish hai: pehle samajh aur yaqeen, phir amal.

## Kitaab kin ke liye hai

- Khaas taur par high school ki aakhri jamaaton aur college ke ibtidaai barson ke naujawaanon ke liye.
- Aam padhne waale bhi is se faayda utha sakte hain.
- Is se pehle ka edition Hindustan ke bahut se madrason ne apni oonchi jamaaton ke nisaab mein shaamil kar liya tha. Yeh edition zaroori kami beshi ke saath shaaya hua.

## Masla kya tha

Aam deeni nisaab ka zyada waqt fiqhi masaail ki tafseel par lagta tha: namaaz, roza, tahaarat aur isi tarah ki cheezein. Aqaaid ko nisbatan kam waqt milta, aur jo padhaya jaata woh dil mein nahin utarta tha. Nateeja yeh ke taalib-e-ilm taleem khatm kar leta magar yeh na jaan paata ke:

- Islam asal mein kya hai aur insaan se kya chaahta hai;
- kyun chaahta hai;
- us ke aqaaid ka insaan ki rozmarra zindagi se kya ta'alluq hai;
- unhein maanne se kya faayda aur na maanne se kya nuqsaan hai;
- Islam apne aqaaid sirf zor se manwaana chaahta hai ya un ki sachchaai ki daleel bhi deta hai.

## Musannif ki daleel

1. **Ahkaam imaan par tike hain.** Jis baat par yaqeen na ho us par koi amal nahin karta, is liye yaqeen ke baghair ahkaam sikhaana be-asar rehta hai.
2. **Imaan samajh par tika hai.** Imaan tabhi mazboot hota hai jab aqaaid theek theek samajh mein aa jaayein.
3. **Tafseel se pehle hikmat.** Namaaz ka tareeqa batane se pehle yeh bataiye ke namaaz kya hai, kyun farz hui, us se kya milta hai aur use chhodne se kya khoya jaata hai. Tareeqa usi ke kaam aata hai jo namaaz padhna chaahta ho. Yahi usool baaqi tamaam ahkaam par bhi laagu hai.

## Is kitaab ka tareeqa

- Is mein Quran Majeed ki ta'leemaat ka khulaasa pesh kiya gaya hai.
- Quran hi ke andaaz mein baat daleel ke saath ki gayi hai, mehaz hukm ke taur par nahin.
- Musannif ise deeni taleem ka ek naya tareeqa kehte hain jo puraane tareeqe se bahut mukhtalif aur maujooda zamaane ke liye zyada mufeed hai, aur ummeed rakhte hain ke is se zyada nateeja-khez deeni taleem ka darwaaza khulega.

Deebaache ke aakhir mein *Abul Aala* ka naam aur 14 Muharram-ul-Haraam 1361 Hijri (1 February 1942) ki taareekh darj hai. Yeh teesre edition (March 1942) se zara pehle ki taareekh hai, is liye ghaaliban yeh deebaacha usi edition ke liye likha gaya aur chauthe mein bhi barqaraar rakha gaya.

## Ghaur karne ki baatein

- Kya main jaanta hoon ke main jo maanta hoon *kyun* maanta hoon, ya sirf yeh ke maanta hoon?
- Kaun si ibaadatein main mehaz aadat se karta hoon, un ka maqsad samjhe baghair?
- Agar mujhe kisi bachche ko Islam sikhaana ho to main ahkaam se shuru karoonga ya un ke maani se?
