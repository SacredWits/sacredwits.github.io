## Khulaasa

Deebaacha chhota hai, magar is mein poori kitaab ki soch simat aayi hai. Maulana ki baat yeh hai ke deeni taleem mein yeh batane se pehle ke Islam *kya* hai aur use *kyun* maana jaaye, yeh sikhaaya ja raha tha ke us par amal *kaise* kiya jaaye. Deeniyat isi tarteeb ko badalne ki koshish hai: pehle samajh aur yaqeen, phir amal.

## Kitaab kin ke liye hai

- Khaas taur par high school ki aakhri classon aur college ke shuru ke saalon ke naujawaanon ke liye.
- Aam padhne waale bhi is se faayda utha sakte hain.
- Is se pehle ka edition Hindustan ke bahut se madrason ne apni oonchi classon ke syllabus mein shaamil kar liya tha. Yeh edition zaroori kami beshi ke saath chhapa.

## Masla kya tha

Aam deeni syllabus ka zyada waqt fiqh ke maslon ki tafseel par lagta tha: namaaz, roza, paaki aur isi tarah ki cheezein. Aqeedon ko un ke muqaable bahut kam waqt milta, aur jo padhaaya jaata woh dil mein nahin utarta tha. Nateeja yeh ke student padhaai khatm kar leta magar yeh na jaan paata ke:

- Islam asal mein kya hai aur insaan se kya chaahta hai;
- kyun chaahta hai;
- us ke aqeedon ka insaan ki rozmarra zindagi se kya taalluq hai;
- unhein maanne se kya faayda aur na maanne se kya nuqsaan hai;
- Islam apne aqeede sirf hukm ke zor par manwaana chaahta hai ya un ki sachchaai ki daleel bhi deta hai.

## Maulana ki daleel

1. **Deen ke hukm imaan par tike hain.** Jis baat par yaqeen na ho us par koi amal nahin karta, is liye yaqeen ke baghair hukm sikhaana be-asar rehta hai.
2. **Imaan samajh par tika hai.** Imaan tabhi mazboot hota hai jab aqeede theek theek samajh mein aa jaayein.
3. **Tafseel se pehle maqsad.** Namaaz ka tareeqa batane se pehle yeh bataiye ke namaaz kya hai, kyun farz hui, us se kya milta hai aur use chhodne se kya khoya jaata hai. Tareeqa usi ke kaam aata hai jo namaaz padhna chaahta ho. Yahi usool baaqi saare hukmon par bhi laagu hai.

## Is kitaab ka tareeqa

- Is mein Quran Majeed ki taleem ka khulaasa pesh kiya gaya hai.
- Quran hi ke andaaz mein baat daleel ke saath ki gayi hai, sirf hukm ke taur par nahin.
- Maulana ise deeni taleem ka ek naya tareeqa kehte hain jo puraane tareeqe se bahut alag aur aaj ke zamaane ke liye zyada faaydemand hai, aur ummeed rakhte hain ke is se zyada asardaar deeni taleem ka darwaaza khulega.

Deebaache ke aakhir mein *Abul Aala* ka naam aur 14 Muharram 1361 Hijri (1 February 1942) ki taareekh likhi hai. Yeh teesre edition (March 1942) se zara pehle ki taareekh hai, is liye shaayad yeh deebaacha usi edition ke liye likha gaya aur chauthe mein bhi rakha gaya.

## Ghaur karne ki baatein

- Kya hum jaante hain ke hum jo maante hain *kyun* maante hain, ya sirf yeh ke maante hain?
- Kaun si ibaadatein hum sirf aadat se karte hain, un ka maqsad samjhe baghair?
- Agar hamein kisi bachche ko Islam sikhaana ho to hum hukmon se shuru karenge ya un ke maani se?
