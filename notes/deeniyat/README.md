# Deeniyat — working notes

Page-by-page points in our own words, collected from the 4th edition (1943) while preparing the
chapter notes published on the site. Not a transcription of the book. Not published.

## Sources
- 4th edition, Pathankot, April 1943 (scan, 156 pp.): preface and contents; page numbers on the site follow this edition.
- Later typeset edition, Idara Tarjuman al-Quran, Ichhra, Lahore (95 pp.): used from chapter 1 onward.
  Has a publisher's note (Husain Farooq Maududi) instead of the author's preface; it states the book
  was first published in 1937 (the 4th ed. lists March 1939 as the first edition), that this is the
  33rd edition, and that translations exist in Arabic, Persian, Indonesian, Swahili, Hausa, English,
  French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Thai, Sinhala, Bengali, Sindhi, Pashto, Gujarati, Hindi, Tamil,
  Malayalam, Danish and Portuguese. Not kept in the repo (copyright).

## What is kept here
Only a short outline per chapter. The detailed page-by-page working points stay outside the repo,
because a near-complete paraphrase of a book still under copyright should not be public.
