# Chapter 1 — Islam — outline (published notes: src/content/posts/deeniyat-01-islam/)

1. Why "Islam": named after a quality (submission to God), not a person or nation.
2. The reality of Islam: the whole universe follows God's law; even the human body submits.
3. Man's two sides: compelled (nature) and free (reason, choice) → complete Muslim vs kafir.
4. The reality and harms of kufr: ignorance, injustice, ingratitude, self-harm, failure in both worlds.
5. The benefits of Islam: freedom as a test; knowledge with direction; character; honour, strength,
   contentment, love, trust; Islam as humanity's natural religion.
