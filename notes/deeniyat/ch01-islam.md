# Chapter 1 — Islam (pp. 9–24) — working points

Sections: Why it is so named (9) · Meaning of the word "Islam" (10) · Reality of Islam (10) ·
Reality of kufr (13) · Harms of kufr (13) · Benefits of Islam (17)

## p. 9
- Chapter opens with Bismillah and lists its six sections.
- **Why it is so named:** almost every religion takes its name either from its founder or from
  the people/nation it arose among. Author's examples:
  - Christianity — named after its founder, Jesus (Isa).
  - Buddhism — after its founder, Mahatma Buddha.
  - Zoroastrianism — after its founder, Zarathustra.
  - Judaism — after the tribe of Judah (Yehuda) in which it arose.
  - Other religions' names follow the same pattern.
- **Islam is different:** its name points to no person and no nation. It names a *quality*,
  the one contained in the meaning of the word "Islam".
- The name itself shows: not invented by one man, not reserved for one nation, no tie to any
  person, country or people.
- Its only aim is to create the quality of "islam" in people.
- Truthful, righteous people of every age and every nation who had this quality were all
  "Muslim" … (sentence continues on p. 10)

## p. 10
- (cont.) … were "Muslim" then, are Muslim now, and will be in future.
- **Meaning of the word "Islam":** in Arabic it means obedience and submission. The religion is
  called Islam because it is obedience and submission to Allah.
- **Reality of Islam:** everything in the world follows a fixed law.
  - Moon and stars are bound by a mighty law and cannot deviate from it by a hair.
  - The earth turns at a set speed, on a set path and timing, with no variation.
  - Water, air, light and heat each follow their own rule.
  - Minerals, plants and animals: each has an appointed law by which it is born, grows,
    shrinks, lives and dies.
- **Man is no exception:** look closely and the human being too is subject to the law of nature.
  - Born by the rule set for his birth; breathes, takes water, food, heat and light by it.
  - Heartbeat, blood circulation, breathing: all bound by the same order.
  - Brain, stomach, lungs, nerves and muscles, hands, feet, tongue, eyes, ears, nose: each
    part does exactly the work assigned to it, in exactly the way assigned.
- This mighty law, binding everything from the greatest planets to the smallest particle of
  earth, is a law made by one supreme Ruler. All … (continues on p. 11)

## p. 11
- (cont.) Everything in the universe obeys that Ruler, because it follows the law He made.
  So the "religion" of the whole universe is Islam, since Islam means obeying God.
  - Sun, moon and stars are muslim; so is the earth; so are air, water, light;
    trees, stones and animals too.
- **Even the denier is muslim in his body:** a person who does not know God, denies Him,
  worships others or associates partners with Him is still muslim by nature and constitution.
  - His birth, life and death all fall under God's law.
  - Every limb and every hair of his body has Islam as its religion, since each forms,
    grows and moves by God's law.
  - Even the tongue he uses to voice shirk and kufr is, in itself, muslim.
  - The head he forcibly bows before others is born muslim.
  - The heart in which, through ignorance, he holds love and reverence for others is muslim
    by nature.
  - Reason: all these obey God's law; every movement of theirs is under it.
- **Now a second angle — man's two sides:**
  1. Like all other creation, man is bound by the mighty rules of nature's law and is
     compelled to follow them.
  2. Unlike them, he has reason: the power to think, understand and form an opinion …
     (continues on p. 12)
