# Chapter 2 — Faith and Obedience — outline (published notes: src/content/posts/deeniyat-02-faith-and-obedience/)

1. Obedience needs certain knowledge of: God's existence, His attributes, His law, the Hereafter.
2. Iman = knowing and accepting with certainty; seed and tree.
3. Four kinds of people by faith and conduct.
4. Where this knowledge comes from: reason's limits; God's kindness through Prophets, wahy, His Book.
5. Iman bil-ghaib: the doctor/lawyer/teacher analogy; trusting the Prophet.
